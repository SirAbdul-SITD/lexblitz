⚡ LexBlitz — The Ultimate Word Scramble Speed Game!

Got fast fingers and a sharp mind? LexBlitz challenges you to unscramble thousands of words across 21 categories — against the clock.

─────────────────────────────────────
🎮 THREE ELECTRIFYING MODES
─────────────────────────────────────

🧩 CLASSIC — Pick a category and power through 100 scrambled words per session. Earn stars, collect coins, and unlock every category.

⛓ CHAIN — Connect words where each answer's last letter starts the next word. Break the chain and lose a life. Ruthlessly satisfying.

⚡ BLITZ — 60 seconds. Maximum words. Build combos, freeze the timer, chase your high score. Pure adrenaline.
